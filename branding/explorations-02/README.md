### 01 - Modular

Use case: logo-brand. Asset type: ONE polished brand concept presentation for OmniLauncher, a premium Minecraft launcher centred on a personal library of games, precise UI and beautiful console-like experience. This is a fundamentally NEW direction: DO NOT use an O ring, broken circle or a tiny notch. Design a distinctive bold compact OM-inspired modular monogram. Build one unified symbol from three substantial softly chamfered masses interlocking with two wide deliberate negative-space channels. The symbol should suggest several game cartridges/modules converging into a single place; it can hint at the m in omni, but must not simply be a typed letter. Seek an inventive overall silhouette: stepped upper contour, grounded lower contour, subtle forward momentum, equal visual weight and a clever interaction between positive and negative space. Keep broad gaps and substantial bridges that survive 24px. Minimal mature premium software identity with real graphic impact, not cute, not busy. Flat filled shape with sharp precise edges, using one warm dusty rose colour around #E68AA6, on uniform warm near-black #151216. NO gradients, NO glow, NO shading, NO 3D, NO texture, NO shadows. Landscape 3:2 presentation, generous editorial negative space, main large symbol paired with the exact wordmark 'omni' in a refined substantial lowercase contemporary sans-serif. In a small lower strip repeat the SAME symbol at three progressively smaller icon sizes in rose and porcelain, so its silhouette is visible when reduced. Text only 'omni' and small '01', no slogans or explanatory paragraphs. No extra decorative brand assets. Avoid stock tech logos, infinity loops, linked chain rings, spirals, letter O, generic cube/prism, gamepad, rocket, sparkle, Minecraft grass block, McDonald's arches, overly thin strokes and microscopic details. Make this feel art-directed and simple enough to remember yet not merely a basic geometric shape.

### 02 - Passagem

Use case: logo-brand. Asset type: ONE polished brand concept presentation for OmniLauncher, a premium Minecraft launcher with console-like clarity, independent game installations and refined desktop UX. Create a fundamentally NEW and inventive logo route with a STRONG unmistakable compact silhouette, not an O or a circle with a notch. Concept: a spatial gateway formed by two bold folded architectural pieces, an abstract passage seen in oblique perspective. Two substantial interlocking angular shapes form a wide central negative-space path that opens diagonally upward; the outside silhouette has confident broad shoulders and one unmistakable stepped/chamfered corner, all structural features large enough to survive an app icon. Suggest entering a world and the tactility of a game cartridge without depicting a literal doorway, house, arrow or Minecraft block. Balance angular cuts with two restrained softly rounded exterior corners. One sophisticated unified graphic mark; not a ring, infinity, cube, prism or generic letter A. Use a single flat uniform warm dusty rose near #E68AA6 and optional porcelain #F5F1ED on a uniform warm near-black #151216. No gradients, glow, glass, shadows, texture or 3D rendering. High-end graphic identity, clear positive/negative space, substantial filled shapes with generous inner openings; creative through silhouette, not tiny details. Landscape 3:2 editorial presentation with generous negative space. Pair the main large mark with the exact lowercase wordmark 'omni' in a precise contemporary sans-serif. Along a small lower strip repeat the SAME mark at three progressively smaller icon sizes to show readability. Only text 'omni' and small '02'; no tagline, no descriptions, no cards or presentation mockup perspective. No orbit, sparkle, chain link, rocket, gamepad, grass block, disconnected confetti or thin lines.
